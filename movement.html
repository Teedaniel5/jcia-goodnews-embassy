🌍 JCIA Movement

The JCIA Movement is the evangelical arm of the Embassy — soul winning, street evangelism, outreaches, missions and open-air crusades marked by preaching the gospel, healing, deliverance, prophecy, miracles and wonders (Mark 16:15-18). It carries the burden of the vision: reviving and repositioning people for total subjection to the Higher power that is God.
