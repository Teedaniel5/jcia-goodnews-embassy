About JCIA — The Good News Embassy

A prophetic ministry birthed in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, and sent to the Nations.

Our Story

Birthed in Bulawayo. Sent to the Nations.

The Good News Embassy is the prophetic house of the Jesus Christ Intelligence Agency (JCIA) — a family of redeemed champions carrying one obsession: the Good News of Jesus Christ. From humble beginnings in Bulawayo, the ministry has carried crusades, teachings and prophetic fire across cities and borders.

The word "intelligence agency" is deliberate: we are heaven's HUMINT in the Earth — gathering God's counsel (Amos 3:7), decoding eternity into daily life, and deploying agents of light into every sphere of society.

Our Vision

Redeemed souls raised to be Kingdom stars of the light in Christ Jesus, thereby repositioned to shine and for dominion worldwide — both in the supernatural and the natural.

Our Mission

Engaging in evangelical Kingdom advancement endeavours, thereby reviving and repositioning people for total subjection to the Higher power that is God by: preaching the gospel of Jesus Christ, healing, deliverance, prophetic, miracles & wonders — Mark 16:15-18.
